Hourly Power Price

See the current electricity price in cents per kWh at a glance. Each price is shown as a number and as a speedometer gauge that runs from green (cheap) to red (expensive) over a 0 to 50 cent range, on your Home Screen, Lock Screen and Apple Watch. Prices come from ComEd's public hourly price feed.

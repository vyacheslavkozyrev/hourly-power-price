FAQ

Why is the number in my widget a few minutes old?

Widgets refresh on a schedule that iOS controls, so the number may be a few minutes old. A 5-minute price older than 15 minutes is dimmed, and a failed update shows a dash.

Why can an alert arrive late or be missed?

Alerts are local and best effort. iOS decides when the app can check the price, so a notification can arrive late or be missed.

What does Premium unlock?

Month statistics, the month price distribution, tomorrow's hourly prices, price history and price alerts. Premium is a single one-time purchase of US$3.99. There is no subscription and nothing renews.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open any Premium screen in the app (for example History) and choose Restore Purchases on the locked screen. Apple restores Premium for the same Apple ID that bought it.

Do I need an account?

No. The app has no account. See the Privacy Policy.
